Panel protection

A picture that stays on for hours can wear the same LEDs. These settings take effect when you press Save.

Moves static pictures one pixel every few minutes.

After this many minutes with no new plane, the clock, weather and idle screens dim to this percent of full brightness, never above your brightness setting. Planes are never dimmed. A new plane or a button press brings the brightness back.

The panel is dark between these local times, using the time zone set below. A start later than the end runs past midnight; equal times mean no blanking. Planes and headlines do not wake it. A short press of UP or DOWN lights it for 30 seconds.

Off by default. Swaps the weather and clock screens every this many minutes when nothing else is showing.

Data service

The address of your ledmatrix-api service, for example apis.example.com. Leave it empty to show example screens. It also needs a location above for flights and weather. There is nothing to enter for access: the display signs every request to your service with a key that belongs to this board, and the service checks it. The key is put on the board over USB and cannot be set here.

Wi-Fi and device




The display is reached at ledmatrix.local and its setup Wi-Fi is called ledmatrix. Change it only if you run more than one display on this network.


Use 1 to 24 letters, digits or hyphens, with no hyphen at the start or end. Capital letters become lower case.


Each display on a network needs a name of its own. If another device there already answers to the same name, the display adds -2 to its own (for example kitchen-2.local). The address line on the display itself does not show that -2, but the IP address above it always works.


Saving a new name restarts the display, which takes about 20 seconds. Afterwards it is reached at  and its setup Wi-Fi is called . This page opens the new address by itself once the display answers there.
